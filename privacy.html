Privacy Policy

Mülk Tüccarı · Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Summary

Mülk Tüccarı is a fictional mobile game about buying and selling real estate. You do not need to create an account or give your name, email or phone number to play. Your game progress is stored only on your device. As the developer, we do not collect or store personal data about you on our own servers. The game contains ads (described below).

2. Developer

Developer: Seyit Cenan (individual developer). Contact: seyitcenan.games@gmail.com

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (money, day, portfolio, settings, language choice, ad-free purchase status) is stored on your device in an area only the game can access. It is not sent to us. When you uninstall the app, this data is removed from the device. If your operating system's backup feature is on (Google backup, iCloud backup), the save file may be included in that backup, which is governed by Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

4. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show ads. Ads come in two forms: rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional interstitial ads between game moments.

To serve, measure and secure ads, the ad service may process information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. This data is processed by Google. How Google uses data from partner apps is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and choices: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, a consent prompt is shown on first launch where required, and personalized ads are requested only with your consent. You can change your ad choices later in the game's settings. On iOS, the system asks for tracking permission ("Allow app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites"); if you decline, the advertising ID is not used. You can also reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

5. In-app purchases

The game offers an optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Your card and payment details are never shared with us. Your purchase status is linked to your store account and can be restored on a new device with the "Restore purchases" option.

6. Children's privacy

The game is designed for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us.

7. Your rights and requests

Because we do not keep personal data about you on our own servers, there is no account for us to delete or correct. For data processed through the ad service, you may exercise your rights (such as access, deletion and objection under the EU General Data Protection Regulation and Türkiye's Law No. 6698, KVKK) with the relevant provider (Google, Apple). For any question or request about this policy, you can reach us at the email below.

8. Third-party services

Apart from the above, the game does not use analytics, social login or location services. If a new service is added in the future, this policy will be updated.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the "Last updated" date will change.
